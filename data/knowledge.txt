# SAMPLE COMPANY HANDBOOK

## Vacation
Full-time employees receive 15 paid vacation days per calendar year. Requests should be sent to a manager at least two weeks ahead. Unused vacation days do not roll over.

## Working Hours
Standard hours are Monday–Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Flexible schedules require manager approval. Lunch is 12:30 PM to 1:00 PM.

## Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval. Everyone must be reachable from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 8 paid sick days annually. A medical note is required after more than three consecutive sick days.

## Expenses
Submit business expenses with receipts within 30 days. Expenses above $250 need prior manager approval.

## Learning
Each employee has a $500 annual learning budget for approved courses, books, and certifications. Manager approval is required before purchase.

## Benefits
The company does not offer pet insurance. Health insurance enrollment information is provided during onboarding.
